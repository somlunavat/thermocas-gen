Matched-bound placement test, launched 2026-11-14, pre-registered in
paper/draft.md section 5.2h (commit da4b6a2, immediately before these
launches).

Corpus: external (uncapped, unbalanced), identical to the 2026-11-10 batch --
byte-identical sequences, clusters and splits. Primary backbone, stage A,
3,000 steps, A10G, Gate 2 on 2,000 validation sequences, five shuffles. These
two runs differ from gext_k2/k4/k6/k9 only in --bin-edges.

WHAT THIS TESTS. Section 5.2g's rank correlation failed and the explanation
offered for it was post-hoc: that k=9 paid because it was the first partition
to cut at 38 C, separating the 18,922 sequences above 38 from the Swiss-Prot
37 C pile, rather than because it was more refined. These two runs test that
account.

run         edges                 k  bound      min mass  min seqs  call id
gplc_nest   27,29,31,35,37,38     7  +0.3154%   9.93%     18,012    fc-01M7EGJMZY48GBSF8KHGWMB3BT
gplc_match  30,37,38              4  +0.2550%   9.93%     19,702    fc-01M7EGJZJ7G24YSJDF7ZRPM9C9

gplc_nest is gext_k6's partition (27,29,31,35,37) with one edge added at 38
and nothing else changed -- a strict refinement, so exactly one variable
moves. gplc_match carries gext_k6's bound (+0.2566%) to within 0.0016 pp
using four classes instead of six, and is the matched-bound test proper.

REFERENCE FIGURES, same corpus, same splits, same 2,000-sequence test cap,

PREDICTIONS BY EXPLANATION, from 5.2h. The reason for two runs is that the two
low-k explanations are indistinguishable on gplc_nest alone.

  explanation      gplc_nest (k=7)        gplc_match (k=4)
  the 38 C cut     near +0.19%            near +0.19% on four classes
  class count      between +0.06 and .19  near gext_k4's +0.07%
  the bound        near +0.19%            near gext_k6's +0.06%

gplc_match is the discriminating run. Class count and the bound predict nearly
the same figure for it (+0.07% against +0.06%) and this design cannot separate
those two.

Primary outcome, fixed before launch: sign of (gplc_nest test gain) minus
(gext_k6's +0.062%), predicted positive beyond two standard deviations and
recovering more than half of the +0.129 pp that k=6 to k=9 bought, so at least
+0.065 pp. Secondary and discriminating: sign of (gplc_match test gain) minus
gext_k6's, predicted positive beyond two standard deviations.

Void conditions: z < 3 on either run, or a rarest class under 5% of residues
(both are 9.93% by construction). A Gate 2 SIZE failure does not void this
test -- both outcomes are differences between runs, not ratios to a bound, and
three of 5.2g's four runs failed on size.

Reports land at checkpoints/stage_a/<run>/{gate2,test}_report.txt on volume
thermocas-gen-vol. Test is scored at the validation-selected checkpoint and
capped at --gate2-limit, so 2,000 of 11,263 -- the cap will bind, as it did on
the 5.2g batch.

Landed 2026-11-14. Wall clock 8,052s (gplc_nest) and 7,754s (gplc_match).
Both selected step 2,500 by validation, as all four 5.2g runs did. Both scored
2,000 of 11,263 test sequences.

EXIT CODES. Both returned 3. That is the Gate 2 failure code, from the
VALIDATION gate failing on size (0.46x and 0.49x against a required 0.50x).
Both runs PASSED Gate 2 on TEST, at 0.62x -- the two highest ratios in this
corpus's series. All four significance checks pass: test z 21.4 and 16.8,
validation z 12.5 and 21.7. Void condition (i) is z < 3 and (ii) is a rarest
class under 5% (both 9.93%), so neither applies, and 5.2h said in advance that
a size failure does not void an outcome defined as a difference between runs.

OUTCOME. Both pre-registered predictions met on both splits.

  primary    gplc_nest - gext_k6  = +0.133 pp (9.8 sigma) on test
                                    +0.044 pp (3.1 sigma) on validation
             predicted positive >2 sigma AND >= +0.065 pp. Measured +0.133 pp,
             which is 103% of the +0.129 pp that k=6 to k=9 bought.

  secondary  gplc_match - gext_k6 = +0.095 pp (6.9 sigma) on test

  measured gains: gplc_nest +0.195% test / +0.144% val

EXPLANATIONS. The cut account survives. Class count is refuted with k held
exactly fixed: gplc_match and gext_k4 are both k=4 and differ only in edge
placement, +0.087 pp at 7.7 sigma. The bound is refuted at a matched bound,
and that is a failure of the bound rather than a success, as 5.2h said in
advance.

Analysis in measured.md, including the decomposition (the cut is worth +0.09
