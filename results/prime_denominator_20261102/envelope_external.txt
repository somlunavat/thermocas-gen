A two-class threshold reaches 30.4% of the envelope.
The conventional labelling therefore leaves a factor of 3.3 on the table -- not in measured gain,
which section 5.1 shows is a different and smaller quantity, but in
what the corpus makes available to any label at all.

Bias is the Miller-Madow correction, (|R|-1)(|B|-1)/2N nats,
converted to the same percentage units. It grows with the number of
classes, which is why the envelope row needs it most.
