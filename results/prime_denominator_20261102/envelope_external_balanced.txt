So the gap between a two-class threshold and the envelope is not
all available to a label anyone could train on. The remainder
sits in classes of a few hundred sequences, and a bound computed
there describes a partition, not an experiment.

Bias is the Miller-Madow correction, (|R|-1)(|B|-1)/2N nats,
converted to the same percentage units. It grows with the number of
classes, which is why the envelope row needs it most.
