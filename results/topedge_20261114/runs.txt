Top-edge test: function or temperature? Launched 2026-11-14, pre-registered in
paper/draft.md section 5.2i (commit 7cd763f, immediately before these
launches).

Corpus: external (uncapped, unbalanced), identical to the 2026-11-10 and
2026-11-14 batches -- byte-identical sequences, clusters and splits. Primary
backbone, stage A, 3,000 steps, A10G, Gate 2 on 2,000 validation sequences,
five shuffles.

WHY THESE TWO. Section 5.2h established that a cut above the 37 C pile is
worth 7 to 10 sigma, but both of its runs used the edge 38, so the function
(remove the modal temperature from the top class) was not separated from the
value 38. These two runs separate them. They also tighten 5.2h's primary
comparison: gplc_nest ADDED a seventh class, whereas these MOVE gext_k6's top
edge and hold class count at six.

gmod_e45  27,29,31,35,45     6  +0.2853%   9.24%     [45,inf)     0.0%        fc-01M7EVHZ7YC29NWGYXZVHSCQ98

Both are gext_k6's partition (27,29,31,35,37) with the top edge moved. Four of
five edges unchanged; gmod_e38 differs from gext_k6 by one degree on one edge.
The two new bounds agree to 0.0016 pp.

PREDICTIONS, fixed before launch.
  primary    sign of (gmod_e38 - gext_k6) on test: positive beyond two
             standard deviations and above +0.09 pp, two thirds of the
             +0.133 pp that 5.2h measured for adding the same edge, since one
             fewer class is available here than in gplc_nest.
  secondary  gmod_e45 agrees with gmod_e38 within two standard deviations
             while both exceed gext_k6 by more than +0.09 pp. Agreement
             within noise is NOT equivalence and will not be called that; it
             licenses only the negative statement that we found no evidence
             the specific value 38 matters.

THE TRANSFER TEST WE RETIRED. 5.2h proposed contaminating pretrain's top class
instead. It is not possible under the 5% floor: the most contaminated top
class pretrain admits is 43.8% modal holding 6.0% of the corpus, and
external_balanced tops out at 38.2%, against external's 71.1% in a class
holding 37.2%. On pretrain the only temperatures whose own mass exceeds the
mass above them are 98 and 99 C, at 0.8% and 0.3%. Recorded in 5.2i as a
finding in its own right: the diagnostic's condition is realizable on one of
our three corpora only, which is evidence it is structural and also means it
cannot be falsified by transfer within this paper.

Void conditions: z < 3 on either run, or a rarest class under 5% of residues
(9.93% and 9.24% here). A Gate 2 SIZE failure does not void this test; both
outcomes are differences between runs.
