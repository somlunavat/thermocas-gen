Corpus: external (UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot reviewed prokaryotes in full, Engqvist
growth temperatures, NO per-species cap and NO species balancing -- the
corpus as the databases have it). 182,522 training sequences, 64,855,763
residues, 77 distinct recorded temperatures. Relabeling only -- byte-identical
sequences, clusters and splits across all four runs. Primary backbone, stage
A, 3,000 steps, A10G, Gate 2 on 2,000 validation sequences, five shuffles.

Envelope for this corpus is +0.4878% corrected, so k=9 reaches 69.2% of it and
k=2 reaches 30.4%. The bound rises 2.28x from k=2 to k=9, against 2.5x on
pretrain (5.2e) and 2.1x on external_balanced (5.2f).

Bounds were recomputed to six decimals before launch rather than read off the
three-decimal envelope table, and balanced_edges was re-run on all four k to
confirm it returns exactly these edge sets. It does.

Sequence populations per class are recorded because the quantile design this
series replaced was abandoned on exactly that check: its smallest classes held
482 and 313 sequences. This is the largest of the three corpora and the
smallest class here holds 13,771, so no run is near the population floor.

WHAT MAKES THIS THE ADVERSARIAL CASE. Refinement on this corpus buys
resolution almost entirely inside the mesophilic pile -- eight of the k=9
edges fall inside twelve degrees and one class holds every sequence above
38 C (48,451 of 182,522). On external_balanced the k=9 edges span 28 to 90.
So this series varies resolution within a narrow band while leaving the
thermophilic tail unresolved, which is not the question 5.2e and 5.2f
answered. It is also the corpus where the statistic already lost once, on
threshold placement in 5.2d.

Reports land at checkpoints/stage_a/<run>/{gate2,test}_report.txt on volume
thermocas-gen-vol. Test is scored at the checkpoint validation selected
(scripts/11_train.py:843) and capped at --gate2-limit (scripts/11_train.py:857),
so 2,000 sequences -- the same cap as the 2026-10-27, 2026-10-30, 2026-11-03
and 2026-11-09 batches, and the test split here is far larger than that cap so
it WILL bind, unlike on external_balanced.
